## 2. Division of Responsibility & Contribution Verification

The conceptual architecture, ethical safeguards, constitutional routing rules, and problem definition were formulated to address real-world governance challenges in Nashik district, Maharashtra.

* **Student Intellectual Contribution:**
  * Formulation of the non-punitive civic listening model and peaceful assembly safeguards.
  * Research and synthesis of Maharashtra administrative statutes (MMC Act 1949, ZP & PS Act 1961, RTS Act 2015).
  * Design of the coarse locality privacy decoupling architecture.
  * Validation and verification of local Nashik taluka and municipal ward structures.
  * Creation of presentation deck contents, demo video narration, and competition submission materials.

* **AI Tool Contribution:**
  * Accelerating coding velocity for vanilla JavaScript DOM manipulation and CSS styling.
  * Automated JSON schema syntax formatting and local HTTP server scaffolding.
  * Synthesizing bilingual Devanagari translation dictionaries.
