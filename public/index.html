Indian Polity, Governance & Public Finance Reference

Source-attributed constitutional architecture of India, 3-tier federalism, Maharashtra & Nashik administrative routing, and public finance flows.

Tier-1 Official Government Sources Only

Constitutional Architecture: Federal Parliamentary Republic

Source: India Code (Constitution of India, Arts. 1, 245-255, 7th Sch.)

India is a parliamentary, federal constitutional republic. The Union, States, and Local Self-Governments operate under statutory mandates defined by the Constitution and state legislative enactments. Crucially, service delivery ownership is governed by departmental jurisdiction and local laws — not by the individual whim of an elected MP or MLA. Civic complaints must be routed to the statutory asset owner (e.g. Municipal Corporation for city roads, PWD for State Highways, Zilla Parishad for rural health).

Tier 1: Union of India (संघ)
Parliament (Lok Sabha / Rajya Sabha) & Union Ministries

National defense, highways (NHAI), railway corridors, Union budget, central tax devolution.

⬇️ 7th Schedule & Article 270 Devolution ⬇️
Tier 2: State Government (महाराष्ट्र शासन)
State Legislature (Vidhan Sabha / Parishad) & Executive Departments

Public health, police/public order, state highways (PWD), state education boards, local government laws.

⬇️ 73rd & 74th Amendments (11th & 12th Schedules) ⬇️
Tier 3: Local Self-Governments (स्थानिक स्वराज्य संस्था)
Urban Local Bodies (ULBs)

Nashik Municipal Corporation (122 Wards), Malegaon Municipal Corporation, 6 Municipal Councils, 8 Nagar Panchayats.

Water supply, city roads, drainage, solid waste, fire services, streetlights.
Rural Local Bodies (PRIs)

Nashik Zilla Parishad (LGD 276) ➔ 15 Panchayat Samitis ➔ 1,380+ Gram Panchayats.

Rural water schemes, primary schools, village sanitation, rural health centres (PHCs).

Public Finance & Intergovernmental Fund Flows

Union Budget & Finance Commission
How taxpayer money travels from the central divisible pool to Nashik local bodies
1

Union Tax Pool Devolution

Union Government collects Gross Tax Revenue. Per the 15th Finance Commission formula, 41% of the net divisible tax pool is devolved horizontally to States.

Maharashtra Share FY 24-25: ₹77,800 Cr (RE)
2

Intergovernmental Grants

Direct statutory grants are transferred under Article 275 for Rural Local Bodies (RLBs) and Urban Local Bodies (ULBs), divided into Tied (drinking water, sanitation) and Untied funds.

Nashik ZP FC Grant: ₹164.5 Cr | NMC Grant: ₹82.3 Cr
3

District Planning Committee (DPDC)

Under Article 243ZD, the District Planning Committee consolidates municipal and rural plans into the District Annual Plan for road networks, tribal development, and public healthcare.

Nashik DPDC Annual Outlay: ₹1,050 Cr

AI & Rights Safeguards Charter

Ethical principles governing CivicLoop: protecting peaceful assembly, preventing surveillance, and ensuring algorithmic accountability.

HSC 2027 Core Mandate
🕊️

1. Peaceful Assembly Protection

Reports concerning strikes, protests, student action, or paper leak grievances are treated as issue-level civic listening. CivicLoop strictly prohibits tracking demonstrators, mapping protest movements, facial indexing, or treating peaceful assembly as a public threat.

⚖️

2. No AI Truth or Guilt Judgments

AI heuristics only suggest classification and route matching. AI output is never evidence of truth or falsity, credibility, guilt, or officer culpability. No automated law-enforcement dispatch or automated public allegations are permitted.

🛡️

3. Identity & Case Record Decoupling

Reporter identities are never coupled to public issue records. We strictly avoid Aadhaar, student IDs, phone numbers, home addresses, faces, or precise GPS coordinates. Sensitive domains default to coarse locality (taluka/ward).

👤

4. Mandatory Human Review for Escalation

Every escalation to senior district leadership requires human authorization with an auditable, written justification. Automated algorithmic escalation is strictly prohibited to prevent grievance inflation or weaponization.

Formal Competition Disclaimers & Limitations

  • Non-Governmental Prototype: CivicLoop is an independent academic prototype created by Team Nav Bharat for HSC 2027. It has no live government server integration.
  • Fictional Demonstration Data: All sample issues, work orders, and response notes are simulated for evaluation purposes.
  • Data Minimization & Deletion: Local demo state is stored ephemerally in browser memory and local storage. No persistent citizen surveillance records are created.
  • Statutory Caveat: Governance references reflect public acts and gazetted sources as of October 2026 and do not constitute formal legal counsel.
